# Website assets

## chamelab-logo.svg, chamelab-logo-transparent.svg, chamelab-mark.svg

The ChameLab logo, two flat colors (gold `#BBA678`, background `#161618`): on a 512 × 512 dark square, on a transparent square, and cropped tight to the mark (site header and home page hero). The app icons, the Android splash icon and the favicons are rendered from it, and the app's top bar uses the same outline (`Logo.chamelab` in `software/chamelab/ChameLab/Styles/Icons.axaml`).
